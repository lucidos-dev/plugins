# Ouroboros

Ouroboros is the classic Snake game, made for Lucidos. Steer the snake, eat the red food, and do not bite your own tail. Each point makes the snake a little faster.

## What you get

- **A game that works at once.** Install it, open it, type your name and play. No account and no setup.
- **Two scoreboards.** "Dagens beste" shows today's best runs. "Legender" shows the best runs of all time.
- **Replays.** Every run on a scoreboard is recorded. Tap a score to watch that run again, move by move.
- **Sound and music.** Music that speeds up with the snake, sound effects, a fanfare for a new record and a sad trombone when you crash. You can turn music and sound off on their own.
- **More than one player.** Each person picks their name before a run, so the whole family can share one board.
- **Phone and desktop.** Swipe or use the on-screen arrows on a phone. Use the arrow keys or WASD on a keyboard.

The game text is in Norwegian.

## What it needs

Nothing, for a local game. Your scores save in your own workspace.

A **shared scoreboard** is optional. It lets friends or family on other Lucidos workspaces play on one board. It needs a Firebase Realtime Database that you own. After install, ask Lucidos to set up a shared board. It adds the proxy for you, and the board then shows up by itself under the 💾 button in the game.

## What to try first

1. Open Ouroboros and type your name.
2. Tap **Start** and play one round. On a phone, swipe on the board to turn.
3. When the run ends, find your score under "Dagens beste" and tap it to watch the replay.
4. Want to play against someone in another house? Ask Lucidos: "Set up a shared Ouroboros scoreboard."
